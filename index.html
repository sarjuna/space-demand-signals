Space Demand Signals

Investors in the space economy say they cannot get good data. This project tests whether the signals people usually reach for instead (public attention, online sentiment, stock prices and policy activity) actually track the real build-out of launches, satellites and capital. It runs the same tests on two very different space economies, India and the United States, using only public data that can be traced to its source.
