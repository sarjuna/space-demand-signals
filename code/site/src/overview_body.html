Space Demand Signals

Is demand for the space economy real, or is it hype? Quarter by quarter, the Space Signals Index compares how much attention people pay to space with how much is actually being built, in India and the United States, using only public data that can be traced to its source.

How to read the numbers: 100 is each country’s own 2016–2025 average, and every 10 points is one standard deviation (a clearly unusual year). A gap within ±5 points is treated as in line. Each value covers the 12 months to the end of the quarter. Each country is measured against its own history, so compare directions, not levels.

LEAD

Who leads whom? Time-series tests

A vector autoregression (VAR) models two monthly series together, so each can be forecast from the recent past of both. A Granger test then asks whether one series’ past improves forecasts of the other. Each pair is tested five ways; a lead counts as robust only if it holds in all five.
