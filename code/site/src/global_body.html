Global LEO filings

Before a country's operators can use radio frequencies for a satellite system, its national administration must file the system with the International Telecommunication Union (ITU). These filings are the earliest public record of planned satellites. This page maps every active ITU filing for a non-geostationary system with orbits in low Earth orbit (apogee up to 2,000 km), by the country that filed it.

Data: ITU Space Networks System, up to BR IFIC No 3084 (10 Nov 2026) Pulled 13 Nov 2026 Scope: active non-geostationary filings with a LEO orbit

The leading filers and the stage they have reached

A filing passes through stages. Advance publication (API) announces a planned system; a coordination request (CR/C) starts negotiations with other operators and fixes the system's priority date; notification asks for the frequencies to be recorded in the Master International Frequency Register. Each bar counts filings by their current notice type.
