Quantum computing

Is the space pattern specific to space? The same tests, run on a second emerging technology: does public attention to quantum computing track its commercial build-out, does one firm dominate, and do investors react to events? The terms, firms, events and tests were fixed in a dated plan before any quantum data were pulled.

Q1

Does attention follow events?

Field Attention Index: mean z-score of log Google Trends (US) for "quantum computing" and "quantum computer", English-Wikipedia reading of the Quantum computing and Quantum supremacy articles (per million total views), and monthly Hacker News stories with "quantum comput" or "qubit" in the title. Firm Attention Index: Google Trends and Wikipedia for the three listed pure-plays, IonQ, Rigetti and D-Wave. Same construction as the space indices.

Q2

Does attention track the build-out?

Quantum computing has no equivalent of the launch catalogue, and qubit counts have no consistent public series. The build-out measures are therefore commercial: quarterly revenue of the four listed firms (SEC filings), federal contract obligations to quantum firms (USAspending), and how often annual reports mention quantum computing.

Bars: revenue, US$ millions (IonQ, Rigetti, D-Wave, Quantum Computing Inc). Line: quarterly mean of the Field Attention Index. Quarters in which all four firms reported, 2022 Q1 – 2026 Q2.

Q3

Does one firm dominate?

In space, one firm (SpaceX) carries most launches, satellites, contracts and attention. The quantum equivalents: shares of listed-firm revenue, of federal contracts, and of Wikipedia reading of the three listed pure-plays.

Q4

Do investors react to events, and do prices lead attention?

Equal-weight basket of IonQ (from October 2021), Rigetti (March 2022) and D-Wave (August 2022), against the S&P 500. Abnormal returns use a market model estimated over the 110 trading days ending 11 days before each event; cumulative abnormal returns (CAR) are in log-return percentage points. The three events and their trading days were fixed in the plan.
